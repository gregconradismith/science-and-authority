<p><em>"I went to college hoping to learn how to think.  Instead I was taught what to think."</em>   This assertion is political.  The speaker is suggesting that the higher educational establishment is overrun by professors who are (inappropriately) using their academic authority to indoctrinate students with respect to social justice issues.  Both the premise of this quote and the ideology it expresses are worthy of discussion, but not today.</p>
