<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Activity</h2>


<p>Think of some famous person who has authority and consider to what extent is that authority is... </p>


<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>traditional</strong>: legitimized by long-standing custom,</li>


<li><strong>charismatic</strong>: based on a leader’s personal qualities, or </li>


<li><strong>legal-rational</strong>: authority resides in the office, not the person.</li>
</ul>


<p>To compare with others, pick three percentages that add to 100% and mark the corresponding point on the triangle.</p>


<div class="wp-block-media-text alignwide is-stacked-on-mobile" style="grid-template-columns:36% auto"><figure class="wp-block-media-text__media"><img src="../../wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1920px-barycentric_rgb.svg_.png" alt="" class="wp-image-3798 size-full" /></figure><div class="wp-block-media-text__content">
<p>green for charismatic</p>


<p>blue for legal-rational</p>


<p>red  for traditional </p>
</div></div>
