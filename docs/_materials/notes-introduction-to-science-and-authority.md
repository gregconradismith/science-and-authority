<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Observations and interpretations</h3>


<p>As we have already noted, the phenomenon of science denial often involves lack of clarity about statements of <strong>fact</strong> and statements of <strong>opinion</strong>.  When the facts themselves are debatable, it is helpful to distinguish <strong>observations</strong> (elementary facts) from <strong>interpretations</strong> (ideas about the meaning of  observations).  </p>


<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />


<h3 class="wp-block-heading">The scientific, the rational, and the true</h3>


<p>This one is my personal favorites.  I think it is very useful to discriminate between three broad categories: the <strong>scientific,  </strong>the<strong> rational, </strong>and the<strong> true</strong>.  For any fact or opinion, any objective or subjective phenomenon, consider where it belongs on a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venn_diagram">Venn diagram</a> in which the sets are imaginary containers for all things scientific, rational, and true.  </p>
