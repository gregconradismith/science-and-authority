<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Prepare for Discussion</h2>


<p>American political discourse is often degenerate.  Socialism and communism are not the same thing, but in the 20th century both systems lead to totalitarian regimes.   Democratic capitalism is our legacy, but some worry that globalized neoliberalism is our future.  Tyranny, dictatorship, and fascism, are all bad, but the words are not interchangeable.   </p>


<p>As we move forward in the course, we will make every attempt to uses these terms from political philosophy accurately, and not slanderously.   Here are two sources that can help.  </p>


<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>This online <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="http://webhome.auburn.edu/~johnspm/gloss/" target="_blank">Glossary of Political Economy Terms</a> by Dr. Paul M. Johnson of Auburn University provides short and sweet answers.</li>
</ul>
