<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Modernization has involved secularization of institutions, but not necessarily a decline in religion </h3>


<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>A longstanding consensus around classical versions of secularization theory has broken down in recent decades.  Religion's stubborn refusal to disappear has prompted a major reevaluation of inherited models of secularization.... The “facts” are not much disputed: New religious movements continue to arise, older movements like Pentecostalism and Mormonism are expanding; religious fundamentalisms thrive throughout the world; and, at least in the U.S., substantial segments of the population continue to participate in orthodox organized religion.  (Chaves 1994)</p>
</blockquote>
</blockquote>
