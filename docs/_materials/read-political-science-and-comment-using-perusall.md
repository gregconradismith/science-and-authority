<p>As described on the blog page <strong><a href="../fall-2023-midterm-project-description/">Midterm Projects</a></strong>, Rian and Collin will be leading a discussion of <strong>Political Science</strong>, Ch 5 in <em>Making Sense of Science: Separating Substance from Spin</em> by Cornelia Dean <a href="https://www.hup.harvard.edu/catalog.php?isbn=9780674237803">[Harvard University Press]</a> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Making-Sense-Science-Separating-Substance/dp/0674059697/">[Amazon]</a> <a href="https://wm.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/permalink/01COWM_INST/1tc7lat/alma991033647902603196">[ SWEM ]</a> <a href="https://ebookcentral-proquest-com.proxy.wm.edu/lib/cwm/detail.action?docID=4830753">[Ebook Central]</a></p>


<p class="has-vivid-red-color has-text-color has-link-color">In addition to reading the chapter, prior to the end of the day Sunday, help jumpstart the conversation in <strong><a href="https://app.perusall.com">Perusall</a></strong>.  </p>


<p><strong>Jumpstart the conversation in Perusall </strong></p>


<ul>
<li>Highlight 3 passages that seem significant to you. Say why this seems like a "take away".</li>


<li>Highlight 2 passages for which you have questions. Say what your question is.</li>


<li>Highlight 1 passage with which you particularly agree or disagree</li>
</ul>
