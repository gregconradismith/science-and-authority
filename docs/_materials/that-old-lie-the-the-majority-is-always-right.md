<p><em><strong>An Enemy of the People</strong></em>&nbsp;is a&nbsp;1978&nbsp;American&nbsp;drama film&nbsp;directed by&nbsp;<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Schaefer_(director)">George Schaefer</a>&nbsp;based on&nbsp;<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Miller">Arthur Miller</a>'s 1950 adaptation of&nbsp;<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henrik_Ibsen">Henrik Ibsen</a>'s 1882&nbsp;play. The film stars&nbsp;<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_McQueen">Steve McQueen</a>&nbsp;in the lead role of scientist <strong>Thomas Stockmann</strong>,&nbsp;<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Durning">Charles Durning</a>&nbsp;as his brother <strong>Peter</strong>, and&nbsp;<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibi_Andersson">Bibi Andersson</a>&nbsp;as his wife <strong>Catherine</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Thomas Stockmann (Steve McQueen) is a doctor and amateur scientist in a small, unnamed Norwegian town. The town is expecting a major boost in tourism due to the therapeutic powers of nearby springs, but Stockmann has discovered they are being polluted by waste from the town's tannery. (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Enemy_of_the_People_(1978_film)">From Wikipedia</a>)&nbsp;</p>
