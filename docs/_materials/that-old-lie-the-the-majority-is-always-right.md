<p><em><strong>An Enemy of the People</strong></em> is a 1978 American drama film directed by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Schaefer_(director)">George Schaefer</a> based on <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Miller">Arthur Miller</a>'s 1950 adaptation of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henrik_Ibsen">Henrik Ibsen</a>'s 1882 play. The film stars <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_McQueen">Steve McQueen</a> in the lead role of scientist <strong>Thomas Stockmann</strong>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Durning">Charles Durning</a> as his brother <strong>Peter</strong>, and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibi_Andersson">Bibi Andersson</a> as his wife <strong>Catherine</strong>.</p>


<p>Thomas Stockmann (Steve McQueen) is a doctor and amateur scientist in a small, unnamed Norwegian town. The town is expecting a major boost in tourism due to the therapeutic powers of nearby springs, but Stockmann has discovered they are being polluted by waste from the town's tannery. (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Enemy_of_the_People_(1978_film))">From Wikipedia</a> </p>
