Biology seminar that may be of interest

This is completely optional for Science & Authority students, but I thought I would pass it along. The first biology seminar of the semester is advertised below.

This week we have a very special guest, Arturo Casadevall, the Bloomberg Distinguished Professor, and Alfred and Jill Sommer Professor and Chair of the Department of Molecular Microbiology and Immunology at Johns Hopkins University. He has won nearly every scientific award imaginable (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arturo_Casadevall), has written popular books on the process of science, and coming fungal pandemics. 

He will present a seminar aimed at a broad audience: Will climate change bring new infectious disease?

In order to accommodate Charter Day activities, the seminar will be in ISC 1127 at 1pm.
