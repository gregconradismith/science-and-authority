<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Clearing</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">by Martha Postlethwaite</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Do not try to serve<br>the whole world<br>or do anything grandiose.<br>Instead, create<br>a clearing<br>in the dense forest<br>of your life<br>and wait there<br>patiently,  until the song<br>that is yours alone to sing<br>falls into your open cupped hands<br>and you recognize and greet it.<br>Only then will you know<br>how to give yourself<br>to the world  so worthy of rescue. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
