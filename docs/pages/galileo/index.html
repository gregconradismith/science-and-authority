Galileo

Based on the play by Bertolt Brecht (originally translated by Charles Laughton), Galileo explores not merely the infamous historical figure, but the philosophical concepts for which he was both celebrated and condemned. "Written and produced in the shadow of the anti-Communist witch hunt that ultimately caused both Brecht and Losey's permanent exit from the United States, this story of the seventeenth-century Italian astronomer – forced by the Catholic Church to recant the scientific discoveries that ran counter to religious doxa – had an obvious timeliness. But in typically Brechtian (and Loseyesque) fashion, this Galileo is no hero. Oscillating between narcissism and cowardice, Galileo instead illustrates the necessity, and difficulty, of ethics...."
