Instructor Notes - What is Authority?

Activity

Think of some famous person who has authority and consider to what extent is that authority is ...

  • traditional: legitimized by long-standing custom,
  • charismatic: based on a leader’s personal qualities, or
  • legal-rational: authority resides in the office, not the person.

To compare with others, pick three percentages that add to 100% and mark the corresponding point on the triangle.

green for charismatic

blue for legal-rational

red for traditional

Fun fact: this is called a barycentric plot.
