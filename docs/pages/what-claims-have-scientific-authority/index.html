What claims have scientific authority?

Any claim that is upheld by professional scientists? Any claim expressed in language and ideas that sound sufficiently similar to scientific theories? Any discussion confined to the natural science domain (such as physics, chemistry, biology, geology)? But then what about the human sciences (such as neuroscience, anthropology, sociology, economics)?
