How to Think

  • Make distinctions
  • Read authoritative authors, then question. The comma is important.
  • Admit your personal convictions. Try to prove yourself wrong, or at least ask yourself what new information would be sufficient to change your mind.
  • Understanding is far more than being `correct.' Topics, like people, need to be approached on their own terms, interrogated in a manner that is consistent with their nature
